# Define the Extension Trust and Update Policy

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05

## Question

How are the first-party extension, optional allowlisted extensions, their permissions, versions, provenance, updates, and per-Profile enablement controlled? Decide what the operator can change, what must be signed or pinned, how risky permissions are surfaced, how rollback works, and how the product prevents one extension or update channel from becoming a cross-Profile credential-exfiltration path.
