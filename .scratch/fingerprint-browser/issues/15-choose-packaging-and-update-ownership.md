# Choose Packaging and Update Ownership

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 05, 09, 10

## Question

Who supplies and updates the Chrome or Chromium binary, the Rust application, the first-party extension, policies, and data migrations on Windows 11? Decide installation privileges, signing expectations, offline behavior, update atomicity, rollback, compatibility pinning, and what happens when browser and control-plane versions drift, while keeping public distribution outside the MVP.
